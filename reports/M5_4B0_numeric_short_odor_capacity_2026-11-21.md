## 6. Mechanistic structural limit and scientific conclusion

The ten-gene expansion is not a freely evolvable RNN: it creates 401 numerical weights with only **ten freely inherited degrees of freedom**, and the rest are fixed by human-selected directional weight-sharing layout. Crucially, the **recurrent weight terms are all fixed to zero**. Consequently the neural genotype cannot maintain a multi-step internal visit-memory state through its recurrent units; it may still condition actions on **current adjacent walls/food, bounded directional odor, current energy, and one-step previous food/failure observations**. Any repeated empty, open, scentless, locally equivalent neighborhood yields the same directional preferences (aside from energy-dependent preference to reproduce), rather than planning unseen exploration using visited-cell memory.

This strong structural restriction makes a competent short-sensor search policy **plausibly difficult to express**; it is a principled follow-up hypothesis **rather than a proven sole cause**. We did not isolate a recurrent-memory intervention in B0. The success of the programmed M5.1 novelty control modifies **both** movement search and birth thresholds, so cannot alone establish that "lack of recurrence" was the single causal driver of numerical extinction.

**What this rigorously demonstrates:** under a fixed preregistered researcher-led 96-candidate external numerical search with independent selection and 36 sealed final worlds, **NO viable short-odor numerical witness was found**, while the human-scripted reference remains reliably viable. The existing ten-gene architecture's representational feasibility at odor2 is **NOT DEMONSTRATED**.

**What it does NOT demonstrate:** that *no possible ten-gene vector can ever survive*; a population-based end-to-end in-world evolutionary mutation advantage; that a neural architecture with true recurrence, stochastic exploration, novelty sensors or different wiring would necessarily fail; that the scripted reference represents learned or evolved intelligence.

**Decision fixed by prospective protocol:** Because the primary representational gate FAILED, do **not** label natural adaptation from random founders successful under this unchanged limited scaffold. In particular, **do not retrospectively widen the optimizer budget, hand-seed a new trained genome in an "evolving" B1 population, or reuse sealed 88200–88223 / 88300–88311 worlds to tune parameters**.
