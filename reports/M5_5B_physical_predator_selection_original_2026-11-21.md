## 1. MAIN (8 source worlds per pressure): pressure intensity changes survival

Each entry means **survivor worlds at tick400 / 8** (not percentage of individual organisms).

| Original physical genetic policy | 0 predators | 1 predator | 3 predators |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Innate nine-gene exact clones, local fear enabled | **8/8** | **8/8** | **3/8** |
| Genuine sparse newborn mutation of 2/9 genes | **8/8** | **8/8** | **4/8** |
| Genuine dense newborn mutation of 9/9 genes | **8/8** | **8/8** | **2/8** |
| Completely random independent nine-gene newborns | **8/8** | **8/8** | **5/8** |
| Exact clone control with enemy sensor and fear response switched OFF | **8/8** | **7/8** | **0/8** |

The original 0-predator clone and fear-blind clone have exactly identical physical behavior, 8/8 survival and mean checkpoint population **13.50**: a valuable exact negative control. One predator captures prey in all eight worlds but does *not* break clone binary survival (8/8); it nonetheless reduces average population compared to no predators. Three predators reduce clone survival to 3/8 and increase meaningful variation among controls: they **remove the ceiling without instant, universal extinction** in MAIN.

The real animal-like predator movement and the local enemy distance field are entirely **engineer-programmed**, not neural intelligence: predators patrol or move one cell toward nearby prey; prey sees only enemies at Manhattan radius2. Their encounters remove the prey from future reproduction and deduct its remaining energy into the original audited death ledger. The ninth genetic coordinate merely changes the weight of an externally supplied avoidance primitive. Original 15 wall/food/odor channels and original food/energy/age/birth mechanics were unchanged.

## 2. Does programmed fear actually matter?

**Yes for these ecological MAIN worlds; it is *not* evidence of evolution.**

- With **one** predator, innate clone mean population at 50-tick checkpoints = **11.917** vs `fear_blind_clone` **9.375**: paired delta **+2.542 organisms**, bootstrap95% **[+1.361,+3.958]**; survival 8/8 vs7/8, paired survival delta +12.5pp with interval [0,+37.5pp].
- With **three** predators, innate clone mean checkpoint population = **7.194** vs blind **2.611**: **+4.583 organisms**, paired bootstrap95% **[+2.694,+6.500]**; physical world survival **3/8 vs 0/8**, +37.5 percentage points, paired bootstrap95% **[+12.5pp,+75pp]**. All eight source worlds have positive clone-minus-blind population effects.
- The fact that blind populations sometimes have fewer **total recorded deaths** is not safety: early extinction simply eliminates opportunities for later captures. Use population trajectories and survival, not cumulative kill count alone.

This is positive causal evidence that the **specified fixed prey avoidance mechanism** protects organisms, and confirms that the enemy model is not a cosmetic fitness penalty.

## 3. Is new heritable mutation already improving fitness?

**No robust main-world evidence yet.** In MAIN with three predators, genuine sparse 2/9 mutation survives in **4/8** vs exact clones **3/8**. Original paired 20k bootstrap survival effect **+12.5pp**, 95% **[−25pp,+50pp]**; population mean checkpoint delta **+0.667**, 95% **[−1.486,+2.889]**. BOTH intervals include zero. Dense mutations fare **2/8**, and **entirely independent random newborn genotypes** survive **5/8**, contradicting any claim that sparse inherited mutation alone produced the observed group ranking.

There were thousands of real legal offspring genetic mutations, but *variation exists* is not the same claim as *new beneficial alleles are being selected*. A change in average living g8 can be genetic drift, founder sorting, ecological demographic conditioning, or genuine selection; these are not resolved by the above aggregate survival outcomes. The ninth gene itself only tunes an already programmed fear rule.

## 4. HARD (4 independent source worlds): avoid overdriving predation

| Policy | 0 predators | 1 predator | 3 predators |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Innate clones | **4/4** | **3/4** | **0/4** |
| Sparse 2/9 mutation | **4/4** | **4/4** | **0/4** |
| Dense 9/9 mutation | **4/4** | **4/4** | **0/4** |
| Random nine-gene newborn | **4/4** | **4/4** | **0/4** |
| Blind nine-gene clones | **4/4** | **4/4** | **0/4** |

Three predators in HARD give **universal extinction** across all five arms, so this is **too severe** for late-generation survival selection. One predator gives an intriguing but fragile HARD sparse-minus-clone population +1.056 [+.778,+1.389] from only four worlds, while binary survival improvement +25pp bootstrap [0,+75pp] is inconclusive; an exploratory small sample cannot justify retroactive promotion to a general adaptation claim.

## 5. Frozen gate, interpretive conclusion and next causal study

The protocol's *diagnostic only* feasibility gate is satisfied **only at three predators in MAIN**: captures occur across the 8 source worlds, clone survival is between 2 and6 of8 (3/8), and real sparse mutated newborns occur in all 8. It is not an evolutionary adaptation claim.

**Supported:** explicit actual predator attacks, physically recorded deaths and balanced energy, repeatable control physiology, local sensory fear with a causal protective effect, new phenotype-dependent selection pressure without immediate universal MAIN extinction.

**Not supported:** that a new mutation is causally beneficial, a genetically new flight behavior evolved, a neural model learned fear, or automatic transferable adaptation. The unexpected competitive random-newborn control requires particular scrutiny.

**Next experiment, independently preregistered:** freeze the MAIN three-predator regime from this pilot, then evaluate source founder g8/genotype vs *actually born modified descendants*, exact descendant-to-founder **gene reversions** and shuffled/random newborn controls in the never-touched 94200–94223 and 94300–94311 independent worlds. Matched world food RNG, enemy spawn/behavior, parental physical birth costs and original total energy must remain audited. Compare descendant relative reproduction and survival, avoiding ancestral-selection leakage; do not train/select candidates using confirmation worlds. Preserve and publish negative results.

The current original result is a **strong proof of useful physical selection pressure**, not a successful demonstration of Darwinian adaptation from mutation.
