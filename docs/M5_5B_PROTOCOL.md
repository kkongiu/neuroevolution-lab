# M5.5B — Prospective feasibility: physically embodied predators as selection pressure

Frozen **before M5.5B implementation and predator-world outcomes**, 21 Nov 2026. This is a separate alternative to the already published M5.5A protocol; **no changes to M5.5A physics, outcomes, files or interpretation**. M5.5A's exact clones survived 24/24 MAIN and 12/12 HARD, leaving binary survival at a ceiling; sparse mutation had no proved improvement. This study investigates whether real enemy encounters create a usable—not universally lethal—fitness gradient.

## Physical threat (not a score penalty)

- Original M5.0 15×15 walls, food, basal energy, age, original legal movements/births, original food schedule and energy ledger remain unchanged. Predation introduces an additional *real death event* with the victim's current positive energy discarded and recorded in the native physical ledger. Each predator can kill **at most one prey per tick** if they occupy the same cell at capture. Prey encounter is checked before enemy movement and after; a successful first attack ends that predator's move for that tick.
- Non-reproducing, immortal, physically located predators: exactly **0, 1, or 3** (frozen pressure levels), each moves at most one cardinal cell per tick, cannot cross walls or share another predator cell. At Manhattan distance ≤4 from any prey, move to a legal neighbour minimizing distance to the closest prey, random seeded tie-breaking. Outside radius4, random legal patrol. Predator position and seeded random decisions are separate from prey-genotype/experimental arm RNG; its route **can react to prey locations**, so not falsely described as an identical trajectory across divergent arms.
- Predator initial positions sampled from accessible cells excluding the initial 20 founder positions with seed-only RNG; same starting positions for each paired arm. Captured prey do not become food, predators cannot change original food regrowth, and organism reproductive/food energy is never waived. Each threat's current location is logged. Capture and movement are separate and auditable.

## Prey: programmed fear sensor and one extra real heritable trait

- Exactly M5.5A's **20 independent random source eight-gene founders** reused for a new study on independent seed worlds. Append **one NEW ninth inherited numeric gene g8**, independently sampled N(0,0.65) per founder. The first eight original M5.5A equations, legal motion, original 15 local food/wall/odor sensors, per-life memory and birth heuristics remain unchanged.
- An added researcher-programmed enemy proximity sense reveals *only* enemies within Manhattan distance2 of the acting prey. Each legal target's risk is the sum of `max(0,(3-Manhattan(target, visible_enemy))/3)` across currently locally visible enemies. Legal move score gains **minus** `(2.5 + 2.5*tanh(g8))*risk` (0..5). No omniscient prey sensor and no emergent discovery of flight; **both the proximity detector and flee rule are human coded**. g8 modifies only its innate strength and can genuinely mutate upon legal births; motor/inheritance costs are unchanged. Reproduction remains M5.5A's original energy-gated rule, creating a trade-off under threat.
- Primary genetic arms: `innate_clone` (exact nine-gene clones), `innate_sparse_mutation` (2/9 independently chosen traits, Gaussian .22), `innate_dense_mutation` (9/9 Gaussian .22), `innate_random_newborn` (all nine independently reset N(0,.65)), `fear_blind_clone` (cloned nine-gene source but disable all predator risk terms). Every mutation occurs only after an actual original-physics legal birth. Blind control retains exact original food and reproduction instincts, so its gap tests the **programmed** danger sense rather than spontaneous evolution.

## Prospective exploratory data, negative controls, independence

- EXPLORE: independent MAIN seeds **94000–94007** and HARD seeds **94100–94103**, 400 ticks × 5 arms × 3 predator levels = **180 original physical world/arm trials** (8+4 worlds, 15 conditions each). Same exact walls, food RNG schedule, source founder genes and predator initial placements for matched arms at fixed (seed, predator count).
- MAIN food regrowth 0.55 / wall density .12; HARD .40 / .20; both odor radius2 and all old M5.5A body physical constants. No optimized source genome or neural controller; do not inspect M5.5A source-world selection.
- Reserved NEW confirmation seeds **94200–94223** and **94300–94311** are NEVER used for this feasibility campaign, and require a separate preregistered confirmation protocol. Do not tune predator count, radius, damage, movement speed, gene mappings, thresholds or world seeds after seeing EXPLORE data. Publish negative and catastrophic outcomes too.
- Summary for each (split, predator count, arm): 400-tick survival fraction, final population, checkpoint mean population AUC, births, food eaten, predation deaths, exposed agents, actual inherited genetic mutations, live g8 mean; match and verify source geometry, predator spawn and founder banks across all five arms. Preserve every predator position/kill event and lineage event for replay.
- For EACH predator count separately compare sparse vs clone (selection on mutated variants), clone vs blind (benefit of programmer-installed sensing), dense vs clone, random newborn vs clone; report world-paired bootstrap (20,000 resamples) for both survival and population AUC. Do not bootstrap individual ticks/organisms as independent worlds.
- **Feasible selection gradient (diagnostic only):** at least one predator condition with real predation events in ≥half MAIN worlds, surviving clone populations in ≥2 and ≤6 of the eight MAIN worlds and at least one true mutant offspring born in ≥half MAIN sparse worlds. Levels which extinguish nearly everything are *not* acceptable evidence of adaptation.
- **Positive adaptive mutation NOT proved by these comparisons alone:** a claimed beneficial new mutation requires independent source-founder vs actual changed descendant transplantation with gene reversion and shuffled/newborn controls in previously untouched confirmation worlds, ideally showing a repeatable lineage advantage at a frozen predator regime. A sparse/clonal population gap, g8 mean drift or survival decrease alone **must not** be called evolved danger intelligence.
