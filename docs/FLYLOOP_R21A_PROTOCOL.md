# R21A — one predator, one prey
Parent R20D 4/4 gates. Keep prey body, R19D motor law, R20B hunger and R20C thermal reflex. Add local directional threat sensing and innate escape only. No learning or new neural weights. Predator is a separate body with frozen recurrent search circuit, local directional prey detection and innate pursuit modulated by hunger. Both sense via line of sight, no global target coordinates passed to controller. Prey detects predator within 3.5 units, predator detects prey within 4.5; omnidirectional 12 sectors, intensity 1-distance/range. Occlusion by solid rectangles. Prey escape blends with ordinary motor at urgency clamp(2*peak,0,1); high urgency drives forward and turns toward cooler/free direction away from threat; thermal reflex retains final priority. Predator speed cap .95 vs prey 1.2, body radius .45 vs .30; attack at contact distance .75 with unobstructed line of sight, four consecutive microsteps, then capture and +12 energy. Predator energy declines .05 per microstep; pursuit weight hunger dependent. No omniscient pathfinding or scripted refuge. Simultaneous commands from pre-step positions, integrate then resolve capture.
Three environments: free, central obstacle, hot lantern plus single food patch. Matched start poses, predator initially 2.5 units behind prey, random global rotation in free/lantern, obstacle placement fixed with randomized poses checked for clearance. Four arms: predator absent, predator present prey blind, predator present escape, predator present escape with no occlusion (sensor diagnostic only; physical capture still occluded). 24 seeds 30100–30123, 20 layouts per scenario, 200 macros (40s), 5760 episodes, 11520 replicated. Record capture, time, survival, food, threat exposure, escape activation, minimum distance, thermal damage, collision, and paired real trajectories. Gates: escape survival >= blind +10pp in free; escape survival >= blind +10pp in obstacle; escape capture lower in lantern; food success in lantern escape >=50% of absent predator; no thermal damage increase beyond 1 point vs absent; absent arm no captures. Report paired seed bootstrap and actual denominators. No tuning or main merge. This is engineered innate flight, not learned or evolved; refuge use requires separate analysis. No reproduction, renewable food, or population equilibrium claims.
