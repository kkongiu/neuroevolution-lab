# M5.4A — Frozen three-axis ecological factor-isolation protocol

**Preregistered 21 Nov 2026 BEFORE writing M5.4A code or opening ANY M5.4A world outcome.** Scientific context: [M5.3C](../reports/M5_3C_genotype_ancestor_transplants_2026-11-21.md) found source-selected descendants (110/120 ordinary) and their true founders (104/120 ordinary) fail ALL harder worlds (0/60 both), without consistent mutation advantage. In previous experiments, shifted environments simultaneously changed wall density .12→.20, odor radius 5→2 and food regeneration .55→.40; three changes were confounded. [Issue #39](https://github.com/kkongiu/neuroevolution-lab/issues/39).

## Causal question and scope

Which of **three** pre-specified ecological stressors—more walls, shorter local food odor, reduced food regrowth—is the strongest causal driver of *failure of FIXED, CLONAL numerical neural genotypes*, including interaction effects? We test all **2×2×2=8** combinations, with paired world maps, initial food, founder positions and presampled food candidate **positions and uniform draws** held constant across the eight treatments.

**This experiment does NOT evolve or train any controller.** Original ten-gene neural genotype architecture is fixed researcher-designed directional weight sharing, not discovered wiring. Five previously source-selected descendant genomes and their own five random-founder ancestor genomes were permanently fixed **before this new study** in [M5.3C source archive](../reports/M5_3C_frozen_candidate_genotypes_2026-11-21.json); same five independent source families **82006, 82015, 82018, 82019, 82023**. Neither genotype vectors nor their selection may depend on M5.4 outcomes.

## Paired physical-world construction (important design change)

M5.0's original `m4_0.make_world` changes its random draws and even the reachable regrowth set when wall density changes, confounding a naive "same seed with two densities" comparison. M5.4A therefore uses an **independent, matched custom world-sampling wrapper** while retaining the ORIGINAL M5.0 wall-collision, 15-channel local sensing, numerical actor, physical birth, energy and food-regrowth laws:

1. **For each new world seed**, use deterministic independent per-cell uniform draws to construct **nested** low/high obstacle walls: high walls are a superset of low walls. Both maps share the same outer border, protected center/neighbors and unchanged 15×15 geometry. Bernoulli thresholds **.12 and .20**; if high map lacks at least **20 reachable founder positions +20 initial food positions**, advance a deterministic map-attempt salt, predecided by geometry only and blind to policy/test outcome; max 100 attempts. Report how many attempts needed.
2. Choose **20 initial food sites** from the high-wall map's accessible cells, then **20 distinct founder sites** from high-map reachable *excluding* food, using fixed independent per-seed RNG. These exact initial food cells and founder positions are then transplanted unchanged to the low-wall map. No founder is placed in a wall/food or unreachable cell. Both maps have the **same food and same founder positions**. More-open low-wall cells may be traversed but receive no exclusive new food, a deliberately conservative matched-resource contrast.
3. Define a **common available food candidate pool** as the high-wall map's center-reachable cells (including any initial food cells). Presample exactly **two food-renewal candidate cells per tick** from this shared pool and the same continuous uniform event marks, **before any organism movement**. For regeneration=.55, candidate event enabled iff mark<.55; for=.40 iff mark<.40. Hence .40 renewal events are a **subset** of .55 events in every world; cells are also exactly the same across all eight arms. Original M5.0 `_food_regeneration` then applies unmodified occupancy, already-food, wall and 32-food-cap rules to these attempts.
4. The odor radius parameter of original M5.0 `observe` is **5 or 2**. Do **not** alter neural input vector (15), neural actor (5 action logits), legal physical action mask, birth energy, costs or lifespan. No inspection of global food position is given to a neural actor; odors remain pre-programmed sensor features.
5. **Critical estimand limitation:** wall-density effect is a controlled *nested-wall intervention with a common restricted food-support pool* and identical initial food/founder positions, not the complete effect of independently regenerating original M5.0 worlds at new density. This deliberately removes initial food map and founder location confounding.

## Frozen 11 organism policy treatments

- **10 genuine numerical neural transplants:** for EACH of five source families use previously frozen **descendant** and its **actual original ancestor**. Each of the 20 founders gets the identical numeric 401-weight M5.3B 10-gene expansion (same pair as M5.3C). Every child is an **exact clone**, no new mutation; all five actions chosen by original unmodified `m5_0.act`. Check on EVERY clonal birth original genotype digest unchanged.
- **One independent scripted positive benchmark `scripted_greedy_novelty`:** original M5.1 local greedy odor + personal visited-cell-count tie-breaks, original threshold-reproduction rule, original physically costed `m5_1._act_scripted`; explicitly label it **human-programmed behavior, NEVER neural evolution**. Script cannot access global food location and must follow original legal motor/birth mask. This provides a check whether the physical ecology remains viable under stronger stressors.

All policies use identical 20-founder initial geometry, 20 food sites, renewal candidate schedule, tick actor permutation RNG, physical costs and 400-tick horizon.

## Fresh independent world partitions and sample units

- **MAIN causal factorial:** **16 NEW world seeds 86000–86015** ×8 factorial treatments ×(10 neural genotype source-family treatments +1 scripted reference) = **1408 world/condition/policy outcomes**.
- **VALIDATION:** **8 NEW world seeds 87000–87007** ×8×11 = **704 additional outcomes**, confirm axis ranking and script viability; no adaptation/fitting after MAIN. Total **2112 complete 400-tick simulations**.
- **Engineering-only smoke:** world seeds 700–701, at most 40 ticks in 11×11 geometry, 8 initial founders, 9 initial food; never cite smoke as scientific world evidence.
- **Randomness replication:** independent world seeds are independent map/food environments. Exactly 5 neural families are the **same previously selected M5.3B successful sources** (thus strong selection conditioning), crossed with 16 main/8 validation worlds. Not 2112 independent evolutionary histories. Inference must resample source families and shared world seeds and acknowledge conditioning. Script arm has no source-family replication (16/8 independent world seeds).

## Outcomes and frozen analysis

Each world-condition-policy row: physical wall counts, accessible cells, map retry count, seed, partition, three factorial stressor flags, source family/policy ID, survival at tick400, population alive, real births, generation max, food eaten/spawned/remaining, early extinctions, actual per-tick food-adjacent and odor opportunities, neural motor-mask corrections, exact energy balance residual, genotype digest, source family and experiment policy origin. Checkpoint mean populations at 0/50/100/150/200/250/300/350/400.

**PRIMARY numerical neural outcome:** average binary tick400 **survival of five DESCENDANT fixed genotypes**, equal weight per original source family and world seed. For each of three axes separately, compute the matched-world **marginal causal difference in survival: stressed minus benign**, averaging over **the four combinations** of the other two axes. For each axis report 5×16 family-world means and the **20,000 crossed family/world deterministic bootstrap** 2.5/97.5 descriptive limits, resampling family IDs and common world seed IDs with replacement, seed **88001** + 101 per axis. An axis is a clearly harmful effect only if its mean<0 and the bootstrap UPPER 95% bound<0. Report rank of effects by absolute mean harm; a factor may only be designated a statistically dominant primary bottleneck if it has a more negative mean than both remaining axes, AND its paired bootstrap for effect difference versus EACH remaining axis has upper bound<0, with 20,000 crossed resamples seed **89101** + predefined index. If no separation, publish **no unique main bottleneck**. Each effect is paired on same source/seed and same nuisance maps/resources. Strict analysis is no post-hoc choice of one single-factor contrast.

**Prespecified secondary contrasts:** the same marginal axis effects for five ancestor neural genotypes and the single scripted benchmark; each four-way pair/three-way triple interaction on descendant survival to expose non-additivity; one-axis-from-baseline comparisons at benign factors (supporting figure only); averaged food-eaten and births for each 8-condition combination with caution against survival-selection effects. Compare script viability on all-stressed corner, requiring ≥4/8 surviving VAL worlds to call physical ecology broadly viable. If script fails this gate, cannot ascribe neuronal OOD failure exclusively to neural search.

**Independent confirmation gate:** use the validation 8 world seeds (same five descendants) to check whether each sign of factor effect is concordant with MAIN, without retuning any weights or physical rule. Report new descriptive crossed intervals with 5000 fixed bootstrap draws; no recursive hypothesis changes. Do not count validation seeds to "improve" a failed MAIN CI unless preregistered and labeled exploratory.

**Factorial interactions:** report pairwise difference-in-differences for 3 pairs of stressor flags over remaining axis, plus three-way interaction, descriptive distribution if meaningful; never label these as individually identified if data insufficient. Survival means with ceiling/floor are allowed and reported. Main study may conclude "no uniquely dominant factor" if effects indistinguishable.

**All-stressed validation:** the all-high walls / short odor / low regrowth corner is *physically different but comparably parameterized* versus M5.3C original SHIFTED ecology because it shares common food/founder maps and food event pools with the paired normal corner; do NOT claim exact reproduction of M5.3C 0/60 or original map generator.
