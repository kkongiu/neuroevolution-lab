# R24A — isolated biomechanics bench
Branch from R23A, no neural or ecological changes. This is a dimensionless planar surrogate, not a calibrated Drosophila simulation. References: Mendes et al. eLife 2013 doi:10.7554/eLife.00231 (representative walking 28 mm/s); treadmill study PMC11461123 (body length ~2.04 mm, sustained 40 mm/s); walking saccades PMC4205811; flight manoeuvres PMC4992712; whole-body simulation Nature 2025 doi:10.1038/s41586-025-09029-4. Real flight includes roll/pitch, sideslip and reverse motion; no claim these are impossible. Our legacy 1.2 units/s and diameter .6 imply only 2 diameters/s; body diameter is not necessarily anatomical length and no physical time conversion is assumed. Do not rescale environment yet.
Compare legacy R12 free-space dynamics against ground and planar air surrogates. Fixed commands (straight, stop, reverse, constant turns at three throttle levels, alternating steering), no sensors or neural controller. Ground: mass=1, moment=.045, linear drag=1.5, yaw drag=.15, forward force=1.8, reverse=.45, yaw torque=.30, lateral acceleration budget=0.8. Ground constraint |v*omega| <= budget via curvature-dependent angular speed projection (idealized no-slip constraint, not full contact dynamics). Air: mass=1, moment=.045, linear drag=.75, yaw drag=.10, thrust=0.9, reverse command brakes along velocity, yaw torque=.20, lateral force limit=.8; planar velocity vector independent of heading, lateral thrust aligns velocity gradually with heading; no altitude/wing model. Air braking cannot reverse velocity by itself; yaw can occur in hover. All parameters illustrative not fitted. Internal integration .005 s, commands held .2 s, report .05 s samples; legacy uses original .05 s. Compare also .0025 s for convergence. Report position, heading, velocity, yaw, lateral acceleration, signed body-axis velocity, force/torque and mechanical work proxy (not metabolic energy). Validate zero input from rest, mirror symmetry, force/torque limits, ground curvature constraint, braking without reversal in straight air case, deterministic replay, numerical convergence. Report no ranking of biological fidelity until calibrated; no ecology integration. Preserve R23A and R23B. Walking/flight differences are separate model families, not modes that organism selects yet.
