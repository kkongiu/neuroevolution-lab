Branch directly from R20D bc337453604d38e548986bd4a634bcb6410586ef. Retain its immediate thermal protection, motor, hunger, metabolism and one-source odor law unchanged. No predator, depletion capacity, sex or learning. Initial energy 90, hot lantern, same geometry and food position as R20D. External schedule fixed before simulation: initial portion12 at tick0; finite control has no further supply; renewable arms add a portion at the same location every 10s or 5s (200 or 100 microticks). Portions accumulate in inventory, one ingestion per microstep, no feedback to schedule from consumption. Same location intentionally isolates temporal supply, not spatial search. Baseline physiological cost per microstep .005+.025+.02*abs(speed)/1.2 = .03–.05, or .6–1.0 energy/s; offer rates 1.2 and 2.4 energy/s exceed maximum metabolic cost but actual ingestion is not assured. Verify observed cost bounds and ledger. No tuning on results. Log schedule hash, initial conditions, supply, consumption, energy, thermal damage, trajectories, repeated departures/returns. These illustrative thresholds are set before execution; report both arms and no automatic promotion.
