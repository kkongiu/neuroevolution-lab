# R22A
Compare one prey + predator, two prey + predator, and two prey without predator. 24 new seeds, 10 layouts, three scenes, 200s horizon, 2160 episodes plus deterministic repeat (4320). Same R21D capacity, thermal and metabolic rules, independently maintained neural states, second prey uses seed+200000. First prey and predator share initial neural weights as R21C. Second starts 2 units laterally from first if clear. Shared finite food (one portion in lantern; none elsewhere), no respawn, no social signals or body-body collision. Predator perceives componentwise maximum of visible individual odor gradients, not a scripted target choice. Existing capture rule, one capture per microstep, no termination upon capture; nearest eligible prey/food recipient, index breaks exact ties. Dead bodies excluded from sensing and integration. Outcomes include per-prey survival, any survivor, first prey survival, capture counts and causes, predator survival and trajectories. No claim of stable ecosystem under finite food. R21D one-prey terminal episode differs by persistence and shared food resolution; matched R22A one-prey control used instead. No preselected directional success gate.
