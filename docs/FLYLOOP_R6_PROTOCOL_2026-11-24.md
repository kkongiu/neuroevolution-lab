# FlyLoop R6 — Preregistered physically intervened training and unseen-world/action transfer
**24 November 2026 · protocol committed before R6 implementation, tests, complete data, or scientific scoring.**

## Why R6 exists
R5 physically imposed alternative motor commands on exact state clones and demonstrated real differences in body and sensor consequences. Yet its sensory prediction head had only observed natural commands in training and failed to predict unseen forced commands strongly enough to beat a same-capacity older-command head. Is **lack of action variety in physical training**, rather than sensory representation alone, responsible?

**R6 trains on actually physically EXECUTED diverse motor actions, then freezes all weights and tests on separate NEVER-SEEN world seeds and command magnitudes.** All training events are physically realized R1-world experiments, not fabricated outcomes. Original R1–R5, sealed M5.6 and main remain unchanged, draft stack only.

## Scientific and ethical limits
R6 is a **researcher-administered, externally trained cross-world transfer of a sensory prediction head**, NOT demonstrated learning inside a single organism's continuous life. The organism itself does NOT select intervention commands or steer from predictions. An experimental head is fitted across multiple training worlds and then transplanted to held-out simulated bodies without motor-policy updates. No foraging, escape, navigation, autonomous agency, intelligence, reproduction or evolution is evaluated.

The hypothesis is about identifying **action-conditioned real physical sensory consequences** under exogenous interventions. Improved prediction is not goal-directed action or an ecological fitness effect.

## Frozen architecture, seeds, geometry, and physical sampling
- Original R1 22×22 physically blocked continuous 2D geometry; radius=.30, timestep=.20, original low-pass two motor actuators, max speed=1.2 and angular speed=1.9, 3 real short-range wall rays and 8 original local sensors, 16 persistent recurrent tanh neural units; EXACT original 434 seeded coefficients, no neural plasticity. Same original R2 σ=.12 seeded motor perturbation sequence per world. No hidden map, global position, route, future ray in the original organism.
- TWO external/identical-form ten-rectangle generators: \`obstacles\` from R1 and \`nearfield\` from R2. No other geometry, no training on test maps.
- TRAIN WORLD SEEDS precisely **10500–10523** (24 seeds ×2 scenes =48 train worlds).
- TEST WORLD SEEDS precisely **10600–10623** (24 seeds ×2 scenes =48 HELD-OUT worlds). No overlap with M0–M4/R1–R5. Test seeds are not visited by any model during training/parameter selection.
- Exactly ONE natural frozen recurrent-motor physical life per seed×scene, maximum 240 frames, with natural pre/post action +16 hidden state +8 sensors stored, NO motor learning. At predetermined frames **40,60,80,100,120,140,160,180,200,220** (ten snapshots per world), freeze the pre-action neural state and full world body/velocity/energy/health/collision memory.
- At every available snapshot physically clone the exact same pre-action body/environment state five times; execute ONE of five investigator FORCED motor commands for **three real physics steps** with the same constant motor; stop on genuine physical death and hold the last real pose as an absorbing physical state. Measure actual **three-step terminal three-wall-ray readings**, body pose, collisions and number of integrations. Never write back clone state to original natural world.
- PREDECLARED TRAIN candidate commands: \`natural\` original actual recurrent motor; \`steer_left\`=(natural throttle, **−0.95**); \`steer_right\`=(natural throttle, **+0.95**); \`throttle_low\`=(**0.10**, natural steering); \`throttle_high\`=(**0.90**, natural steering).
- PREDECLARED TEST candidate commands use NEVER-TRAINED intervention magnitudes: \`natural\` original unforced command (baseline exception); \`steer_left\`=(natural throttle, **−0.55**); \`steer_right\`=(natural throttle, **+0.55**); \`throttle_low\`=(**0.30**, natural steering); \`throttle_high\`=(**0.70**, natural steering).
- Planned counts, if each natural life survives until all snapshots: **96 original physical lives**, **960 snapshots**, **4,800 physically executed three-step branches** (2,400 training +2,400 heldout), **3,840 actual forced-vs-natural sensor contrasts** (1,920 train+1,920 test). Any actual death truncation must be logged honestly; DO NOT resample seeds, discard negative trajectories, or silently relax exact-count integrity gates. A dead experimental clone remains terminal without resurrection and its terminal sensors are computed at the last physically realized pose, identified as censored.
- No snapshot selection by reward, sensory effect, collision, predicted error or survivorship. All data from all predetermined available snapshots archived, including environmental geometry.

## Frozen training method (head-level ONLY; original organism policy never updates)
Three external predictor heads, **84 coefficients each**, same R4 28-feature tensor (16 actual hidden recurrent activations +3 current local wall rays +2 motor command +6 ray×motor interactions +constant 1), predicting the **THREE actual terminal wall rays AFTER THREE physically executed steps** by \`clip(current_ray + dot(weights,features),0,1)\`. All coefficients initialized zero (persistence). EXACT same LMS as R4: \`w += 0.014*(actual_three_step_ray−prediction)*feature\`, clips each weight to [-2,2]; 1 chronological single pass through training rows **scene order obstacles then nearfield, ascending seed, then ascending snapshot tick, then natural/left/right/low/high**, no shuffling, epochs, model selection or heldout-derived tuning.
- \`actual_action\` head: features contain EXACT command physically executed for this sample.
- \`motor_blind\` head: all motor command and six interactions exactly ZERO, but same initial 84 coefficient slots; fewer active parameters, so disclose limitation.
- \`shuffled_action\` head: same 84 coefficient slots and SAME multiset of TRAIN executed motor actions as actual head, but at each snapshot rotate the five actually executed motor commands by one position (natural sample trained with left motor, left with right, right with low, low with high, high with natural). Thus all five commands are **actually executed in independent real clones of exactly the same state**, but features are MISPAIRED to the actual physical sensory target. No fictitious label, no extra randomness, no future observation used by a policy. This shuffled-action head is a researcher-defined adversarial control, not a biologically plausible synapse.
- All three heads see **identical physically realized target observations** for the same training sample and the same nonmotor features. All three have exactly one update per realized training trial; no motor readout updates, ever.
- Freeze ALL 3×84 predictor weights at the end of training. In heldout test worlds NEVER update them, the 434 original neural coefficients, or the physical motor policy. All test data is acquired only after frozen heads exist and is scored without feeding test labels into weights.
- On EVERY heldout forced physical action score one-step-*style* model prediction (but **three-step** physical endpoint, not one-step) against the actual measured three-step wall rays: true vs blind vs shuffled, and exact persistence baseline. Also calculate contrasts of each non-natural forced sample against natural on SAME exact cloned initial state, comparing actual delta of terminal wall rays to actual-head predicted delta and zero-delta baseline.
- Informativeness rule frozen: a heldout forced-vs-natural contrast is informative iff **max absolute REAL three-step terminal ray difference ≥0.02**, not selected on model score. Report both ALL contrasts and informative-only; separate world-family results.
- External physical controllability rule: left-vs-right forced THREE-step final xy separation **≥0.04**. Counts missing snapshots as count failures, never as success. All experiments execute actual physical R1.actuator integration; no action in an internal learned planner.
- Primary inference unit for uncertainty: **24 independently heldout world seeds** paired across two scenes, *not thousands of correlated frames*. Exploratory bootstrap 95% for per-seed true-minus-blind/shuffled prediction error. Preserve actual balances/failed episodes.
