# M5.35 — prospective protocol: truly new synthetic families AND sequential knowledge retention

**Frozen 23 November 2026 before the first full M5.35 GitHub Actions scientific evaluation.**

## Motivation and falsifiable hypotheses

M5.34 showed an improvement from a shared, *supervised* 32→64→16
neural representation across unseen **whole functions within four
already-known research-programmed Boolean families**. It had 3,152
float32 parameters and 96,000 old-family TRAIN episodes per run;
it was not general intelligence or independent FAMILY replication.

M5.35 asks TWO deliberately separate questions:

**H1 — zero-shot new-family generalization:** Does that identical
old-trained representation predict genuinely unseen synthetic
functions generated by wholly new Boolean grammars (not just new
instances in familiar grammars) from eight chosen-action/reward
observations? Report against random and old M5.32 neural baselines.
There is no promise of success.

**H2 — sequential transfer and forgetting:** Once the *same*
representation learns these novel families through another block
of supervised previous-task episodes, can replay of former tasks
preserve performance on heldout original-family functions while
also learning heldout new-family functions? Compare full new-only,
half-novel unique exposure and 50:50 old-task replay under matched
gradient/update/parameter budgets; do not automatically claim
continual autonomous learning.

## Two genuinely new mathematical generator FAMILIES

The entire old M5.34 112-function exact 16-bit truth-table
collection is kept as a forbidden set.

- **MUX:** four sensory bits; choose one of four selector bits,
  choose two ordered distinct other input bits as branches,
  independent branch inversion and output inversion. The
  returned action is a conditional choice of one branch.
  Exhaustive enumeration yields **96 unique truth tables**,
  **zero overlap with old 112**.
- **EXACT-K / signed cardinality:** count active bits after
  a four-bit input-inversion mask, output one precisely for
  cardinality k in {1,2,3}, optionally invert output.
  Exhaustive enumeration yields **48 unique truth tables**,
  **zero overlap with old 112 or MUX 96**.

These primitives are entirely **engineered by researchers** and
supply a new distribution shift relative to old family training;
they do NOT prove natural-world generalization.

Deterministic new-family shuffle \`numpy.default_rng(3535)\`,
sorted unique 16-bit table signatures. Freeze the unique
function-level splits:
- MUX: **40 new TRAIN**, **16 new HELDOUT**, **40 completely unused**.
- EXACT-K: **20 new TRAIN**, **12 new HELDOUT**, **16 unused**.
All **60 novel training functions, 28 novel heldout and 56 unused
novel truth tables are disjoint from each other and old 112**.
The fixed old-family M5.34 split remains **85 old TRAIN/27
old HELDOUT**. None of the 55 TEST functions (27 old +28 new)
enter either old pretraining or new-stage adaptation, even under
replay.

The **zero-shot benchmark** is evaluated before the network
receives ANY training data, labels or function identity from
either new generator. This is a valid *new synthetic-family*
challenge, but not a real-world independently constructed dataset.

## Experiment design and evidence partitions

Exactly **two independently initialized networks**, seeds
**1301 and 1303**, with old phase:
- identical M5.34 ordinary tanh MLP, 32→64→16 and **3,152
  actual float32 parameters**, shared across all old/new tasks;
- 3,000 Adam steps, batch 32 (~96,000 fully supervised OLD
  TRAIN-function episodes per seed) using old M5.34 code
  unchanged, and no heldout or new family labels.

Before any adaptation, frozen evaluation on ALL 27 old and
28 new heldout full functions, with **two fixed random support
episodes** per function and optimizer seed. Each episode:
- 8 distinct observed sensory cues;
- random *precommitted* chosen binary action per cue;
- only chosen action's genuine environmental success/failure;
- exactly 8 never observed cue labels reserved for frozen
  scoring. Same support/exam cues and actual rewards for
  every model variant at every checkpoint. No
  learned active curiosity; no engineered symbolic new-family
  posterior is used in any candidate learner.

To compare truly zero-shot behavior, include the original
M5.32 independently fitted 15-parameter dual-soft network
from exactly the same test supports (8×300 support-only SGD
steps) as a transparent old architecture baseline; it is
**not** parameter, pretraining exposure or FLOP matched.

### Second phase: same checkpoint, equal adaptation steps

Clone precisely the same old pretrained network weights into
three arms; initialize a **fresh and identical** Adam state for
each (no Adam optimizer moment carry-over from old phase):
- **N: NEW-ONLY 100%** — 1,200 Adam steps ×32 entirely new
  TRAIN-family episodes per step (38,400 novel episodes).
- **H: HALF-UNIQUE NOVEL CONTROL** — 1,200 steps ×32,
  sample 16 novel episodes and duplicate their examples
  once within each batch. This has **the SAME 19,200 unique
  new-family episode draws** as the replay arm, while
  applying the full 32-example gradient batch and no old
  examples. Correlated duplicate samples lower diversity.
- **R: 50:50 OLD REPLAY** — 1,200 steps ×32, 16 novel TRAIN
  episodes and 16 old TRAIN episodes, with the **same
  novel episode stream/draws as H** (19,200 unique draws)
  and 19,200 old replay episodes.
  
All arms have **identical 3,152-parameter architecture,
old-model starting weights, Adam reset state, optimizer
hyperparameters (lr .003, beta1=.9, beta2=.999,
eps1e-8), 1,200 update count, fixed batch size 32**.
Per-step random masks observe eight randomly chosen bits
of each old or new TRAIN table; query-loss targets come
from the other eight truth entries of **TRAIN functions only**.
Old and novel TEST function tables remain fully excluded
from both phase training and early selection.

The replay policy is fixed **50:50 by researchers**. Replay
requires keeping old training data and is NOT spontaneous
neural memory. The H arm isolates the impact of old replay
rather than only different novel unique exposure.

**Evaluation:** freeze all weights after 1,200 steps, measure
unseen8 accuracy on the exact SAME saved episodes
both before and after, by function FAMILY and by stage.
Report **forgetting = old after − old before**, negative
means loss. Report **new transfer = new after − new before**.
No retraining on test supports for the shared model.
Calculate exploratory paired bootstrap intervals by unique
heldout truth-function identity (episodes and seeds correlated);
do not treat the 220 evaluation rows as 220 independent
functions.

Pre-declared evaluation size: (27 old +28 novel) functions ×
two actual random episodes ×two old-model initializations
= **220 function/episode/initialization rows**, each with
8 genuine supports and 8 disjoint frozen unseen queries.
Four shared-network snapshots (before, N,H,R) plus the
independently task-adapted dual-soft benchmark in each row.

The pilot/test suite may check a subset of training steps
without touching the final train/test function split or the
full data before the full run; such a unit test is NOT an
independent scientific evaluation.

## Eleven prospective narrow DEV gates (no retuning)

1. **All 28 novel heldout whole functions are disjoint** from
   old M5.34 112 truth tables and the new 60 TRAIN functions.
2. Exactly 96 unique MUX and 48 EXACT-K synthetic truth
   tables, pairwise disjoint from old 112 and each other.
3. Replay post-adaptation **novel mean unseen8 accuracy >=60%**.
4. Replay novel unseen8 improvement vs the pre-adaptation
   zero-shot snapshot **>=+8 percentage points**.
5. Novel-only unseen8 improvement vs zero-shot **>=+10pp**.
6. Replay **old heldout** aggregate unseen8 retained to within
   **5pp below** the pre-adaptation score or better.
7. Replay old heldout unseen8 beats NEW-ONLY old heldout
   unseen8 by **>=+5pp**.
8. Replay old heldout unseen8 beats HALF-UNIQUE NOVEL
   control by **>=+4pp**.
9. Replay novel heldout score is no worse than NEW-ONLY novel
   score by **more than 10pp**, preventing an apparent
   trivial retention win from simply refusing new learning.
10. Verified exact SAME frozen support/exam episodes across
    arms; 8 real support chosen-action/reward pairs, 8
    never-seen cues, identical 3,152-float32 topology,
    parameter bytes and all three stage-two 1,200-step
    training budgets plus novel-draw-matched H vs R.
11. Full raw rows, old/novel per-family subgroup scores,
    both new and old before/after deltas, negative results,
    complete float32 checkpoints, fingerprints and
    prospective evolution-stop flags archived.

**All eleven must pass for the narrow DEV screen**, and all
can legitimately fail. A passing screen is NOT a license
to evolve, to change sealed M5.6, or to claim autonomous
life. Repeated old heldout functions from M5.34 are a
**known previously evaluated benchmark**, NOT an
independent old-family generalization confirmation.
Full report must preserve limitations of small sample
sizes (MUX 16 and EXACT-K 12, vs old family four
strata with only one parity test function).
