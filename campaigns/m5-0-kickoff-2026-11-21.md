# M5.0 — First persistent ecology campaign

The preregistered [M5.0 protocol](../docs/M5_0_PROTOCOL.md) was committed to GitHub BEFORE implementation, CI and all 71000+/72000+ final ecology results. Founders: 20 identical independent random neural genotypes per world; no outside selection of champions or numerical task fitness. Each live agent controls four legal moves and an explicit reproduction action. Birth transfers 12 energy to an actual offspring at parental cost 20+1 basal. Heritable mutation, random offspring and clonal inheritance run on **same 12 main ×400 tick worlds** (71000–71011) and **same 8 shifted ×400 tick worlds** (72000–72007) with precomputed food candidate events. No-food control first 4 main seeds must starve and reproduce never. Primary birth advantage inherited mutation − independent random offspring, paired bootstrap resampling worlds, 9/12 wins and lower interval >0 necessary; additional generation≥3 in 9/12 worlds. Report extinction, failed reproduction, zero births and all null/negative outcomes unchanged. Preserve M4.x code/results. CI runs with read-only repository permissions and archives seeds, energy ledger, lineage events and SVG maps.
