# M4.1 — Frozen replicated masked-navigation campaign

Protocol preregistered before implementation and sealed evaluation: [docs/M4_1_PROTOCOL.md](../docs/M4_1_PROTOCOL.md). Eight independent genetic initializations 81000–81007. Each produces masked-trained and unmasked-trained champions from identical initial genotype populations, with identical evaluation budgets, unique train worlds 45000–45035 and fixed validation 46000–46003. Freeze selected champions BEFORE evaluating 24 unseen main worlds 47000–47023 and 12 shifted worlds 48000–48011. Primary: hierarchical paired food advantage of masked-evolved policy over wall-avoiding random, resampling genetic seeds AND world seeds; require >0 lower bootstrap and ≥6/8 positive independent genetic runs. Secondary: masked random RNN, strong greedy odor, within-genotype mask crossover, no-odor ablation, null-world physics. Keep M4.0 unchanged, preserve negative results, no post-hoc retuning.
